Your favorite athlete closes in for a victorious win. 你最喜欢的运动员正逐渐接近一场胜利。⭐ close in for = 逼近；逐渐接近｜victorious = 获胜的；胜利的
The crowd holds its breath, and, at the crucial moment, she misses the shot. 观众屏住呼吸，而就在关键时刻，她却投失了这一球。⭐ hold one's breath = 屏住呼吸｜at the crucial moment = 在关键时刻｜miss the shot = 投球/射门未中
That competitor just experienced the phenomenon known as "choking," where despite months, even years, of practice, a person fails right when it matters most. 这名运动员刚刚经历了所谓的"关键时刻失常"现象：尽管经过数月、甚至数年的训练，一个人却偏偏在最重要的时刻发挥失败。⭐ phenomenon = 现象｜choking = 压力下失常；关键时刻掉链子｜right when it matters most = 恰恰在最重要的时候
Choking is common in sports, where performance often occurs under intense pressure and depends on key moments. 这种压力下失常的现象在体育运动中十分常见，因为运动表现往往发生在巨大压力之下，并取决于一些关键时刻。⭐ under intense pressure = 在巨大压力下｜depend on = 取决于
And yet, performance anxiety also haunts public speakers, contestants in spelling bees, and even world-famous musicians. 然而，表现焦虑也会困扰公开演讲者、拼字比赛选手，甚至世界知名的音乐家。⭐ performance anxiety = 表现焦虑｜haunt = 持续困扰；萦绕｜spelling bee = 拼字比赛
Most people intuitively blame it on their nerves, but why does being nervous undermine expert performance? 大多数人会直觉地把这种情况归咎于紧张，但为什么紧张会削弱专家级的表现呢？⭐ intuitively = 凭直觉地｜blame something on something = 将某事归咎于某事｜nerves = 紧张情绪｜undermine = 削弱；损害
There are two sets of theories, which both say that primarily, choking under pressure boils down to focus. 对此有两类理论，而两者都认为，从根本上说，压力下失常归结于注意力问题。⭐ choking under pressure = 压力下失常｜boil down to = 归结为；核心在于
First, there are the distraction theories. 首先是分心理论。⭐ distraction theories = 分心理论
These suggest that performance suffers when the mind is preoccupied with worries, doubts, or fears, instead of focusing its attention on performing the task at hand. 这些理论认为，当大脑被担忧、怀疑或恐惧占据，而不是把注意力集中在眼前的任务上时，表现就会受到影响。⭐ be preoccupied with = 满脑子想着；被……占据｜focus one's attention on = 将注意力集中于｜the task at hand = 手头的任务；当前任务
When relevant and irrelevant thoughts compete for the same attention, something has to give. 当相关和无关的想法争夺同一份注意力资源时，总有一方不得不做出让步。⭐ compete for = 争夺｜something has to give = 总有一方必须让步；不可能两全
The brain can only process so much information at once. 大脑一次能够处理的信息量毕竟是有限的。⭐ process information = 处理信息｜only so much = 只有这么多；存在上限｜at once = 同时
Tasks that challenge working memory, the mental "scratch pad" we use to temporarily store phone numbers and grocery lists, are especially vulnerable to pressure. 那些对工作记忆要求较高的任务尤其容易受到压力影响；工作记忆就像我们用来暂时存放电话号码和购物清单的"大脑草稿纸"。⭐ working memory = 工作记忆｜scratch pad = 草稿板；临时存储区｜be vulnerable to = 容易受到……影响
In a 2004 study, a group of university students were asked to perform math problems, some easy, others more complex and memory-intensive. 在2004年的一项研究中，一组大学生被要求完成数学题，其中一些较简单，另一些则更复杂，对记忆的要求也更高。⭐ memory-intensive = 对记忆要求很高的
Half the students completed both problem types with nothing at stake, while the others completed them when calm and under pressure. 一半学生在没有任何得失压力的情况下完成了两类题目，而另一半则分别在平静状态和压力状态下完成这些题目。⭐ with nothing at stake = 没有什么利害关系；没有得失压力｜at stake = 处于风险中；事关得失｜under pressure = 在压力下
While everyone did well on the easy problems, those who were stressed performed worse on the more difficult, memory-intensive tasks. 虽然所有人在简单题目上都表现良好，但那些处于压力中的学生在更困难、对记忆要求更高的任务上表现更差。⭐ memory-intensive = 对记忆要求很高的
Explicit monitoring theories make up the second group of explanations for choking under pressure. "显性监控理论"构成了解释压力下失常的第二类理论。⭐ explicit monitoring = 显性监控｜make up = 构成；组成
They're concerned with how pressure can cause people to overanalyze the task at hand. 这些理论关注的是压力如何导致人们过度分析眼前的任务。⭐ be concerned with = 关注；涉及｜overanalyze = 过度分析｜the task at hand = 当前任务；手头的任务
Here, the logic goes that once a skill becomes automatic, thinking about its precise mechanics interferes with your ability to do it. 这里的逻辑是，一旦某项技能变得自动化，刻意思考其精确的动作机制反而会干扰你完成它的能力。⭐ the logic goes that = 其逻辑是；其观点认为｜precise mechanics = 精确的动作机制｜interfere with = 干扰；妨碍
Tasks we do unconsciously seem to be most vulnerable to this kind of choking. 那些我们无意识完成的任务似乎最容易受到这种压力下失常的影响。⭐ unconsciously = 无意识地｜be vulnerable to = 容易受到……影响
A study on competitive golfers compared their performance when instructed to simply focus on putting as accurately as possible, versus when they were primed to be acutely aware of the mechanics of their putting stroke. 一项针对竞技高尔夫球手的研究比较了两种情况下的表现：一种是被要求只专注于尽可能准确地推杆，另一种则是被预先引导去高度关注自己推杆动作的具体机制。⭐ be instructed to = 被要求；被指示做｜versus = 与……相比｜be primed to = 被预先引导去；被预先刺激以｜acutely aware of = 高度意识到；敏锐地意识到｜putting stroke = 推杆动作
Golfers usually perform this action subconsciously, so those who suddenly tuned in to the precise details of their own moves also became worse at making accurate shots. 高尔夫球手通常是在潜意识中完成这一动作，因此，那些突然开始过度关注自己动作精细细节的人，反而更难打出准确的球。⭐ subconsciously = 下意识地；潜意识地｜tune in to = 开始密切关注；留意｜precise details = 精细细节
Choking may not be inevitable for everyone though. 不过，对所有人来说，压力下失常也许并不是不可避免的。⭐ inevitable = 不可避免的
Research suggests that some are more susceptible than others, especially those who are self-conscious, anxious, and afraid of being judged negatively by others. 研究表明，有些人比其他人更容易出现这种情况，尤其是那些非常在意自己表现、容易焦虑，并害怕受到他人负面评价的人。⭐ be susceptible = 易受影响的；更容易发生某种情况｜self-conscious = 过度在意自己表现的；不自在的｜be judged negatively = 受到负面评价
So, how can we avoid choking when it really counts? 那么，我们怎样才能避免在真正关键的时候发挥失常呢？⭐ when it really counts = 在真正关键的时候
First, it helps to practice under stressful conditions. 首先，在有压力的条件下进行练习会有所帮助。⭐ under stressful conditions = 在有压力的条件下
In a study on expert dart players, researchers found that those who hadn't practiced under stress performed worse when anxious, compared to those who had become accustomed to pressure. 在一项针对专业飞镖选手的研究中，研究人员发现，与那些已经习惯压力的人相比，没有在压力下练习过的人一旦感到焦虑，表现会更差。⭐ under stress = 在压力下｜become accustomed to = 逐渐习惯于
Secondly, many performers extol the virtues of a pre-performance routine, whether it's taking a few deep breaths, repeating a cue word, or doing a rhythmic sequence of movements. 其次，许多表演者都极力称赞赛前或表演前固定流程的好处，无论是做几次深呼吸、重复一个提示词，还是完成一系列有节奏的动作。⭐ extol the virtues of = 极力赞扬……的优点｜pre-performance routine = 表演/比赛前的固定流程｜cue word = 提示词｜rhythmic sequence = 有节奏的一系列动作
Studies on golfing, bowling, and water polo find that short rituals can lead to more consistent and accurate performance under pressure. 针对高尔夫、保龄球和水球的研究发现，简短的仪式化动作能够带来在压力下更稳定、更准确的表现。⭐ ritual = 固定仪式；仪式化行为｜lead to = 导致；带来｜consistent performance = 稳定的表现
And thirdly, researchers have shown that having an external focus on the ultimate goal works better than an internal focus, where someone is tuned into the mechanics of what they're doing. 第三，研究人员已经证明，把注意力放在最终目标这一外部焦点上，要比把注意力放在自身动作机制上的内部焦点更有效。⭐ external focus = 外部注意焦点｜ultimate goal = 最终目标｜be tuned into = 高度关注；专注于｜mechanics = 运作机制；动作机制
A study of experienced golfers revealed that those who hit chip shots while focused on the flight of the ball performed significantly better than those who focused on the motion of their arms. 一项针对经验丰富的高尔夫球手的研究发现，那些在打切球时专注于球飞行轨迹的人，表现明显优于那些专注于自己手臂动作的人。⭐ reveal = 揭示；表明｜chip shot = 高尔夫切球｜the flight of the ball = 球的飞行轨迹｜significantly = 显著地
So, perhaps we can modify that age-old saying: practice, under pressure, with focus, and with that glorious end goal in sight, makes perfect. 所以，也许我们可以改写那句流传已久的格言：在压力下练习，保持专注，并始终把那个令人向往的最终目标放在眼前，才能造就完美。⭐ age-old saying = 流传已久的格言｜end goal = 最终目标｜in sight = 在视野中；（引申）作为明确目标｜practice makes perfect = 熟能生巧
